1. Exact coordinates with a fixed highest space and fixed zero kernel
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Let W_{n,M} have the normalized planar occupation basis indexed by partitions
A=(j_1,...,j_n), sum j_i=M. The spherical weight-M space uses the identically
labelled normalized occupation basis; Q>=16 makes its labels identical.
The following identification is an identification of coordinate vector
spaces and is not asserted to intertwine the two physical inner products
after the further diagonal map below.

Define the positive diagonal matrix D_{n,M,Q} on W_{n,M} by

  d_A = product_{j in A} sqrt(product_{l=0}^{j-1}(1-l/Q)).

The normalized spherical orbital, written in x=sqrt(Q)v/u after removal of
u^Q, is d_j x^j/sqrt(j!). Thus D converts physical spherical occupation
coefficients into the coefficients of the same affine polynomial in the
normalized planar monomial basis. It is an invertible linear map, not a
unitary map. Products with a zero particle output use D=1.

Let L be the planar center-of-mass lowering operator, and J_+ the sphere
operator that decreases orbital weight. Directly on an occupation basis,

  J_+/sqrt(Q) = D_{n,M-1,Q}^{-1} L D_{n,M,Q}.                 (1)

Choose any orthonormal column frame U for ker L in W_{r,z}; U itself need
not be computed for the proof. Then V_{Q,0}=D_{r,z,Q}^{-1}U is a bijective
frame for the spherical highest space. All following finite forms are
pulled back to the SAME coefficient space C^m by this frame, where
m=dim ker L. Their positivity is invariant under this congruence.

For the pair annihilator B_p from W_{n,M} to W_{n-2,M-p}, define

  e_{p,Q}=sqrt(product_{l=0}^{p-1}(1-l/(2Q))).

The exact Clebsch-Gordan formula gives the identity

  B_{p,Q}=e_{p,Q}^{-1} D_{n-2,M-p,Q}^{-1}

Consequently B_{p,Q} V_{Q,0}x=0 for all p if and only if
B_{p,infinity} Ux=0 for all p. The nullspace

  N=ker(U^* H_{r,infinity} U)

in C^m is EXACTLY the nullspace of V_{Q,0}^*H_{r,Q}V_{Q,0} for every

or a perturbatively chosen spherical kernel.

Every unaveraged K_3,K_4 term retains a pair annihilator on the right and
