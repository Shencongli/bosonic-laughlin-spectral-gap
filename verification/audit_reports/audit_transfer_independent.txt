1. Normal ordering, body order, and the support bounds
---------------------------------------------------
Fix a single row with channel t and write

  F = lambda B_t + sum_A alpha_A a_{i_A}^dagger a_{j_A} B_{p_A},
  A=(p_A,j_A), T_A=p_A+j_A, i_A=T_A-t, t<=T_A<=8.

The B operators are physical pair annihilators on the sphere or plane; their
coefficients do not enter the following CCR calculation. For real coefficients,
the exact unaveraged normal-ordered contributions are

  C2 = lambda^2 B_t^dagger B_t;

  C3 = lambda sum_A alpha_A
       [B_t^dagger a_{i_A}^dagger a_{j_A} B_{p_A}
        +B_{p_A}^dagger a_{j_A}^dagger a_{i_A} B_t]
       + sum_{A,B: i_A=i_B} alpha_A alpha_B
         B_{p_A}^dagger a_{j_A}^dagger a_{j_B} B_{p_B};

  C4 = sum_{A,B} alpha_A alpha_B
       B_{p_A}^dagger a_{j_A}^dagger a_{i_B}^dagger
       a_{i_A} a_{j_B} B_{p_B}.

Only a_i a_{i'}^dagger crosses in the cubic-cubic product, producing one
contraction. There are therefore no fifth- or higher-body coefficients and
no extra two-body contributions. Both creation and annihilation orbital sums
in every C3 term equal T_A (the contraction imposes T_A=T_B). Thus its total
weight support is <=8. In C4 these sums equal T_A+T_B-t<=16-t<=16. These
claims are exact at finite Q, once the finite orbital labels exist (Q>=8).

For an r-body operator supported on total weights M<=D, its SU(2) average
has no spin-deficit block z>D: a spin J=rQ/2-z has no weight M<z. This
explains why finite orbital support before averaging becomes a finite spin
cutoff after averaging, despite the averaged operator having descendants
at arbitrarily large M as Q grows. It is not legitimate to say that the
averaged operator still has orbital-weight support <=D; the manuscript's
support statement is understood as referring to its unaveraged coefficient.

2. Exact zero-kernel preservation: it is not an assumption about a limit
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Every term in C3 and C4 has a pair annihilator at the far right and a pair
creator at the far left. If psi is in ker H_{r,Q}, positivity of
H_{r,Q}=sum_p B_p^dagger B_p implies B_p psi=0 for all p. Hence C3 psi=0
or C4 psi=0 exactly, with no norm estimate. SU(2) rotation preserves the
pair space V_Q and ker H_{r,Q}; every rotated coefficient therefore also
annihilates the kernel. The spin projections used in E_r commute with H_r.
H_3^2-H_3 kills ker H_3, and the S4 coefficient is a sum of (B_q B_p)^dagger
(B_q B_p), so it too kills the H_4 kernel. Consequently both E_{r,Q} and
E_{r,Q}+delta H_{r,Q} kill the relevant kernel exactly.

The claim does NOT require S4 to be strictly positive on the whole H_4
image, nor any equality between the H_4 image and the pair-pair image.
The wide map V_Q tensor Sym^2 U_Q -> Sym^4 U_Q has image ran H_4;
the narrower pair-pair map can have a smaller image. The manuscript avoids
equating these, which is essential.

3. Divisibility and rank stability, including the finite-Q degree cap
------------------------------------------------------------------
For a pair of spin-Q/2 particles, restriction of the pair polynomial to
coincident spinors is the multiplication intertwiner to the spin-Q module.
It is nonzero on the unique maximal-spin summand and zero on every other
summand. Thus the V0 pair projector annihilates a state iff the polynomial
vanishes at this coincidence. In a local affine chart, boson symmetry makes
the polynomial even in the relative coordinate, so vanishing implies a
second-order zero. Equivalently the homogeneous determinant
d_ij=u_i v_j-u_j v_i divides it twice. Each d_ij is irreducible and distinct
factors are coprime in the polynomial ring. Therefore all pair constraints
are exactly equivalent to divisibility by Delta_r^2=product_{i<j} d_ij^2.

Delta_r^2 is SU(2)-invariant. It has degree 2(r-1) in each particle and
total v degree r(r-1). For Q>=2(r-1), multiplication by it is an injective
SU(2) intertwiner from symmetric residual polynomials of per-particle
degree Q'=Q-2(r-1) onto the zero-mode space. A spin deficit z in the
physical degree-Q space corresponds to deficit w=z-r(r-1) in the residual
space. For z<r(r-1), the zero space in that weight and relative block is zero.
For z>=r(r-1) and Q>=z, Q'>=w follows from

  Q'-w = Q-z+(r-1)(r-2)>=0.

Thus the finite orbital cap on the residual polynomials is inactive. For
fixed z and Q>=z, physical highest-weight multiplicity is
d_r(z)=p_r(z)-p_r(z-1), and zero-mode multiplicity is
d_r(z-r(r-1)), with d_r(negative)=0. The active rank is therefore exactly
d_r(z)-d_r(z-r(r-1)), equal to the planar rank. The same conclusion for
small Q<2(r-1) and z below the shift is immediate because there are no
zero modes; only the sufficiently-large-Q regime is needed for the proof.

Saved exact H ranks independently matched this formula in every block:
 r=3, z=0..8:  [1,0,1,1,1,1,1,1,1]
 r=4, z=0..16: [1,0,1,1,2,1,3,2,4,3,5,4,6,5,7,6,8].

4. Fixed coefficients really define the same limiting certificate
----------------------------------------------------------------
The JSON gives y0=lambda*kappa_t and
y_pj=alpha_pj*kappa_p*sqrt(j!/i!), with kappa_p^2=p!/2^(p+1).
Inverting these finite nonzero scale factors gives fixed real algebraic
lambda and alpha, independent of Q and N. On each sphere one inserts these
same physical coefficients into F with the spherical B_p^Q. One must not
reuse y as physical spherical coefficients in the polynomial basis; the
manuscript does not do that. In a fixed complete occupation-weight sector,

  binom(Q,i) binom(Q,j) / binom(2Q,p)
       -> p!/(2^p i!j!), p=i+j.

All relevant matrices therefore converge entrywise and in operator norm.
Normal-order extraction is a finite algebraic operation, and its support
has already been proved independently of Q. No limiting exchange involving
growing N or all orbital weights occurs at this step.

5. Highest spaces, descendants, and the Schur factor 2/r
-------------------------------------------------------
Let J_+ lower total orbital weight and J_- raise it. On a fixed occupation
sector, J_+/sqrt(Q) -> L and J_-/sqrt(Q) -> L^dagger. The limiting L has full
row rank: [L,L^dagger]=r implies L L^dagger >= r I on the lower-weight
sector. Thus highest-weight kernels have constant dimension and admit
convergent orthonormal frames for large Q.

In spin J=rQ/2-z, the normalized k-th descendant is

  (J_-)^k psi / sqrt(k! * (2J)_k),

where (2J)_k is the falling factorial. After rescaling by sqrt(Q) per
lowering step, its step-k normalization tends to sqrt(r k). This gives
(L^dagger)^k/sqrt(r^k k!) as in the exact checker.

For an unaveraged r-body coefficient C commuting with J_z, normalized Haar
averaging is identity on the spin factor times its partial trace divided
by 2J+1. Multiplying by d_pair=2Q+1 therefore gives the multiplicity block

  (2Q+1)/(rQ-2z+1) * sum_{M=z}^{D} U_Q(M)^dagger C_M,Q U_Q(M).

The limit factor is 2/r. There is no missing r!, binomial(N,r), or pair
factor 1/2 here. Those combinatorics have already been fixed by the positive
lift convention; L_r restricted to r particles is the identity on r-body
coefficient matrices. At body order two, Schur averaging of
lambda^2 |v_t><v_t| times (2Q+1) is exactly lambda^2 P_{V_Q}. Summing rows
therefore gives precisely eta H_Q at every Q.

6. Positivity with a moving kernel
---------------------------------
After the preceding continuous choices of highest frames, H_Q and
S_Q=E_Q+delta H_Q are finite Hermitian matrices converging in norm. Stable
rank is essential: norm convergence alone would permit tiny new positive
H eigenvalues and could fail to control E there. Here stable rank excludes
that failure. A contour separating zero from the finitely many positive
eigenvalues of H_infinity gives a continuous spectral projection P_Q onto
ran H_Q. One can transport an orthonormal frame of ran H_infinity to ran
H_Q, for example by the polar part of P_Q restricted to the limiting image.

The exact planar PSD test, exact preservation of ker H, and equal ranks
imply that S_infinity restricted to ran H_infinity is positive definite.
Its smallest eigenvalue in an orthonormal active frame is strictly positive.
Hence the transported active S_Q remains positive for all sufficiently
large Q. S_Q kills ker H_Q exactly by point 2, so no uncontrolled off-diagonal
kernel-image block remains. This proves E_Q>=-delta H_Q already; the stated
2*10^-6 allowance is weaker and valid. The positive smallest eigenvalue may
be very small; that affects the unknown Q threshold, not the argument.

7. Independence from total particle number and the plane limit
------------------------------------------------------------
Only r=3,4 and finitely many fixed deficits enter the continuity argument.
Their common flux threshold is therefore independent of the later particle
number N. Positive normal-ordered lifting preserves finite coefficient
inequalities without any norm bound that grows in N. The separately proved
coherent bounds absorb the lifted H_r Pi errors into H_Q and S4_Q with
fixed constants. Their thresholds, plus the tail threshold, are also
independent of N. The final budget calculation has no omitted N factor.

For the final sphere-to-plane implication, first fix N and total degree D.
For Q>=D the whole weight sector exists on the sphere and its pair
Hamiltonian matrix converges to the plane one. Matrix polynomial continuity
passes H_Q^2>=H_Q/3 to the limit. Positivity then extends by direct sums in
D and N on the appropriate quadratic-form domain. This does not choose
Q as a function of N inside the original uniform spherical inequality.
