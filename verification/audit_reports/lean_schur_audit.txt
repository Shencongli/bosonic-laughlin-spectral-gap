Scope

DescendantSchur.lean proves that commutation with the actual J+ makes the
equal-level normalized descendant forms constant. This follows by moving
J- off the left vector with its already proved adjoint and applying the
physical ladder recurrence. No Hermiticity of the tested operator is needed.
The sum has exactly m+1 levels, where m=N*Q-2*d>=0. Its normalization is
1/(m+1), including m=0. The tensorWeight adjoint and real eigenvalue
orthogonality prove that commutation with 2Jz removes unequal-level entries.
For a complete physical highest-weight frame, the resulting commutant block
is delta_(n,l) times the full multiplicity form matrix. Arbitrary complex
coefficient vectors and nonorthonormal multiplicity frames are retained.

Schur identification is conditional: the candidate averaged operator must
commute with J+ and preserve the spin partial trace for every pair of frame
columns. This is a matrix-valued condition, not equality of a single scalar
trace. The finite sum is then identified with the highest-weight form matrix.
The verifier coordinate formula follows from the preceding physical
normalized-descendant theorem; every rho_n inverse has the previously proved
positive denominator for n<=m and Q>0. The exact rational dimension-ratio
identity is algebraic. It does not prove the coherent integral's 2Q+1 factor.

SpinPartialTrace.lean proves the change-of-basis formula U^dagger K U for
two independent spin-indexed physical frames. The cyclic trace identity
and U U^dagger=I give trace preservation. Finite weighted conjugations
preserve the trace if their complex weights sum to one; this theorem makes
no positivity claim about such weights. A physical pullback theorem uses
explicit hypotheses that the same unitary spin matrix acts on both ladders.
No completeness of the entire many-body spin decomposition is assumed.
