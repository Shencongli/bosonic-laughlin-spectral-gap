1. FROM HIGHEST-WEIGHT MATRICES TO FULL PHYSICAL SPIN BLOCKS

Suppose a head run at q_i establishes, for all Q>=q_i and each retained z,

  E_{r,z,Q}+delta_{r,z}(q_i) H_{r,z,Q} >=0.

These are physical multiplicity-space matrices. Because E_r and H_r are
rotationally invariant, the corresponding full spin-isotypic operators
are identity on the spin representation tensor those matrices. Hence
the certified inequality holds on the complete physical isotypic block.
If the computational certificate uses only the active image of H_r, it
must also establish exact kernel annihilation by E_r; the existing normal
ordering argument supplies precisely that fact. The operator-level input
is then valid on the entire block, including its kernel.

2. THE AUXILIARY WEIGHTED PROJECTOR AND THE CREATION MAP

For a fixed r and Q, use one common coherent kernel A_{r,Q}, with one
common a_r. Let lambda_{r,z}(Q)>0 be its proved lower eigenvalue on ALL
auxiliary multiplicities at spin deficit z. Different theta_z are allowed
because they only prove lower bounds for this same kernel.

For nonnegative scalar delta_z,

  sum_z delta_z Pi^{aux}_z
     <= max_z[delta_z/lambda_{r,z}(Q)] A_{r,Q}.

This is a whole-space operator inequality: each full auxiliary spin block
is retained, and the kernel remains positive on all omitted blocks.
No projection onto a chosen multiplicity subspace is being inserted.

The equivariant creation map W_r obeys W_r W_r^*=H_{r,Q} exactly, and
intertwines the full spin projections. Consequently

  W_r(sum_z delta_z Pi^{aux}_z)W_r^*
     =sum_z delta_z H_{r,Q} Pi^{phys}_z.

This identity uses neither injectivity nor isometry of W_r. Applying the
positive r-body lift and the exact coherent occupation estimate gives
cost max_z delta_z C_{r,z}(Q), where C_{r,z} already contains the h=r-2
spectator marginals. In particular the four-body 1/2 spectator-Fock
factor was already used in the exact bound 2S4+H; it must not be inserted
again. There is no further dimension, r!, or binomial factor.

Thus choosing

  delta_{r,z}(q_i)=L_r/Cbar_{r,z}(q_i)

with Cbar uniform for every Q>=q_i proves the desired lifted errors
L_3(S4+H) and L_4(2S4+H), respectively. The scalar is a maximum over z,
not a sum, because the weighted projector is compared to one kernel and
lifted once. All zero-rank physical blocks are harmless.

Thus A_i>=1/3 and B_i>=0 are the correct two rational budget conditions.
No extra c9 or mu term remains. Since K_Q and S4,Q are positive, these
conditions imply H_Q^2>=H_Q/3 on the interval, uniformly in total N.
