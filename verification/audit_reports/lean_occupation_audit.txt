1. Exact coherent-pair containment

In the orthonormal one-particle basis i=0,...,Q, use a normalized complex spinor
(u,v), |u|^2+|v|^2=1, and beta_i=sqrt(choose(Q,i))*u^(Q-i)*v^i.
The binomial theorem gives sum_i |beta_i|^2=1. Define c_p similarly at degree
2Q. For i+j=p the identity beta_i beta_j=t_p(i,j)c_p is exact, and the
product beta tensor beta is the sum of c_p times the normalized pair vectors.
The vector c is normalized. This is the concrete reason a coherent double
occupancy lies in the V0 pair channel, and holds for every integer Q>=0.

Implemented and Lean-checked in BosonicLaughlin/CoherentPair.lean:
coherentCoefficient_normSq, coherentOrbital_normalized,
pairCoefficient_sqrt_choose, coherentCoefficient_pair_factorization,
coherentOrbital_pair_expansion, coherentOrbital_pair_witness.
These results need no SU(2) covariance theorem and impose no threshold on Q.

2. Occupation inequality and normalization

For a normalized orbital beta, B_beta=a_beta^2/sqrt(2), and exactly
B_beta^dagger B_beta=n_beta(n_beta-1)/2. Since beta tensor beta is a normalized
vector in the pair-projector image, its rank-one projector is bounded above
by the V0 projector. Summing over unordered tensor slots yields
H_{Q,M} >= n_beta(n_beta-1)/2 for every particle sector M. No factor M,
Q, or 2 is missing. Coherent-pair containment is necessary here; the claim
does not hold for an arbitrary normalized orbital without that containment.

For each nonnegative integer occupation n, n <= 1+n(n-1)/2. Equality occurs
at n=1,2; it is strict at n=0 and n>=3. Functional calculus in an orbital
occupation basis therefore gives n_beta <= H_{Q,M}+I. More generally the
manuscript's C_eta=max_{n>=0}[n-eta*n(n-1)/2] is finite for eta>0 and gives
n_beta <= eta H + C_eta I. Its eta=1 value is exactly 1.

3. Sector typing of the sandwich

B_alpha maps the N-particle physical sector into the (N-2)-particle sector.
For N>=2, the correctly typed inequality is
B_alpha^dagger n_beta^(N-2) B_alpha <=
B_alpha^dagger(H_{Q,N-2}+I_{N-2})B_alpha.
The manuscript explicitly places its occupation inequality in this output
sector. Sectors N=0,1 have zero pair annihilator and must be handled directly
when formalizing all N; natural-number subtraction alone does not define
their annihilation map.

Pair-frame integration gives integral B_alpha^dagger B_alpha=H_{Q,N};
and integral B_alpha^dagger H_{Q,N-2} B_alpha=S_{4,Q,N}, because pair
annihilators commute. The sphere measure is mass one with pair-frame factor
2Q+1. These are operator equalities requiring their own formal proofs.

4. Two possibly nonorthogonal coherent spectators

With s=<beta,gamma> and annihilators antilinear in orbital coefficients,
[a_beta,a_gamma^dagger]=s. For A=a_gamma a_beta,
A^dagger A=(n_beta n_gamma+n_gamma n_beta)/2
             -Re(s a_beta^dagger a_gamma).
The noncommuting square (n_beta-n_gamma)^2 is positive and gives the required
symmetrized product bound; no commutativity assumption is needed. Quadratic
form Cauchy--Schwarz gives +/- Re(s a_beta^dagger a_gamma)
<= |s|(n_beta+n_gamma)/2 <= (n_beta+n_gamma)/2. Thus
A^dagger A <= (n_beta^2+n_gamma^2+n_beta+n_gamma)/2
<= 2H+n_beta+n_gamma <=4H+2I.
No normalization error was found in this calculation.

The normalized Sym^2-to-Fock isometry sends P_sym(beta tensor gamma) to
a_beta^dagger a_gamma^dagger|0>/sqrt(2), also for beta=gamma. Consequently the
lifted integrand has factor 1/2, giving the manuscript's final 2S4+H rather
than 4S4+2H. The manuscript states this factor explicitly.

5. Economical route for the remaining Lean occupation proof

For a general normalized beta, define its one-slot rank-one projection P_i.
The P_i commute for distinct tensor slots. Define n_beta=sum_i P_i; then
n_beta(n_beta-I)/2=sum_{i<j}P_i P_j. One can prove n_beta<=I+sum_{i<j}P_iP_j
by decomposing into joint occupancy eigenspaces, or by an arbitrary
single-particle unitary taking beta to a chosen basis orbital and tensoring
that unitary. This change of basis only concerns the occupancy projectors:
it does not require the Hamiltonian to be invariant under an arbitrary
unitary. Separately, coherent pair containment supplies V0>=P_beta tensor
P_beta in each pair. Together these give the desired operator inequality.

An alternative polynomial positivity certificate is available on commuting
projections: joint subset projectors E_S=product_{i in S}P_i
product_{i notin S}(I-P_i) resolve identity, and the difference
I+sum_{i<j}P_iP_j-sum_iP_i equals
sum_S [(|S|-1)(|S|-2)/2] E_S with nonnegative scalar coefficients (including
the separate |S|=0 case). This avoids constructing a unitary completion.

7. Independent semantic audit of the assembled occupation module

Reviewed ProjectionOccupation.lean, CoherentOccupation.lean, ModeForms.lean,
PairAnnihilation.lean, NumberAnnihilation.lean, and BosonicSymmetry.lean after
their implementation by the other agents. This section supersedes the first
two outstanding obligations listed at the end of item 6.

ProjectionOccupation proves I-n+C >= 0 for every finite commuting family of
orthogonal projections, where n=sum_i P_i and C=sum_{i<j}P_i P_j. Its induction
uses the exact decomposition
I-(P+A)+(PA+C)=(I-P)(I-A+C)(I-P)+P C P.
No orthogonality between different P_i is assumed, no large-N estimate is
used, and no N-dependent constant is introduced. Hermiticity, idempotence,
and commutation are proved for the actual slot-projector matrices before
the abstract result is used. projection_factorial_moment additionally
proves n*n-n=C+C, with precisely the unordered-pair count of the manuscript.

ModeForms proves the exact Gram identities of the slot-projector number and
pair forms by bijective reindexing of the finite tensor basis. Its spectator
space excludes exactly the contracted slot(s). No dimension, factorial, or
symmetrizer factor is dropped. In particular the full-space expectation of
P_beta,i P_beta,j is the sum over spectator configurations of the squared
two-slot contraction. Combining it with CoherentBound ties C to the actual
pair-projector Hamiltonian from Model.lean, rather than to an auxiliary
Hamiltonian or an assumed majorant.

CoherentOccupation defines n_beta explicitly as sum_i |beta><beta|_i and its
real quadratic form using the finite orthonormal tensor coordinate inner
product. Its coherent_occupation_bound proves
<psi,n_beta psi> <= <psi,H_{Q,N} psi> + ||psi||^2
for every Q,N, every normalized complex spinor, and every full tensor state.
This stronger full-space statement is legitimate; a missing bosonic
hypothesis here is not a weakening or error. BosonicSymmetry separately
proves that the actual H preserves the physical symmetric subspace.

PairAnnihilation defines a_alpha by sqrt(N+1) times contraction of one first
slot and B_alpha by sqrt(choose(N+2,2)) times contraction of the first two
slots. On normalized symmetric tensor coordinates these are exactly the
standard bosonic annihilation normalizations. The algebraic identity
B_alpha=a_alpha a_alpha/sqrt(2) is itself formally proved, as is preservation
of permutation symmetry by each annihilator. The coherent_occupation_pair_lift
is then the preceding all-state inequality evaluated at B_alpha psi, with
input particle number N+2 and output particle number N. Allowing arbitrary
alpha is valid: the sandwich inequality does not require alpha to be coherent
or normalized. The occupation orbital beta is still explicitly coherent and
normalized. This is the quadratic-form statement of
B_alpha^dagger n_beta B_alpha <= B_alpha^dagger(H_{Q,N}+I_N) B_alpha.
It does not merely postulate an arbitrary map bearing the name B_alpha.

NumberAnnihilation closes the first-/second-quantized correspondence for the
number operator: projectionNumber_eq_annihilate_normSq proves exactly
<psi,n_beta psi>=||a_beta psi||^2 for every symmetric input state with N+1
particles, including sqrt(N+1). No beta-normalization condition is needed for
this identity. Its proof uses permutation invariance to equate every slot
contribution and the exact N+1 slot count. The vacuum is already included in
the first-quantized inequality; the typed annihilation map is defined only on
nonempty sectors, which is explicit rather than a hidden natural-subtraction
convention.

8. Final audit of Fock and pair-occupation correspondences

Reviewed the completed FockCorrespondence.lean and the completed source of
PairOccupationAnnihilation.lean. No normalization or scope issue was found.

FockCorrespondence defines B_p on input particle number N+2 by multiplying
the contraction against the normalized real pair vector t_p by
sqrt(choose(N+2,2)). The coefficient is exactly
sqrt((N+2)(N+1))/sqrt(2), matching the manuscript's
B_p=(1/sqrt(2))*sum_{ij}t_p(i,j)a_i a_j in normalized symmetric tensor
coordinates. The pair label runs through every p=0,...,2Q; no orbital or
pair channel is silently omitted. The real t_p coefficient is its own
complex conjugate, so the contraction has the correct bra convention.

The proof first uses bosonic symmetry to equate every unordered pair's
expectation to that of slots 0 and 1, counts choose(N+2,2) unordered pairs
exactly, and uses the full pair Gram decomposition for those slots. This
establishes energy_eq_sum_v0PairAnnihilate_normSq:
<psi,H psi>=sum_p ||B_p psi||^2
for every symmetric input psi in the actual N+2-particle model. Bosonic
symmetry of B_p psi is also proved. This is precisely the quadratic-form
version of H=sum_p B_p^dagger B_p; it is not a theorem about a differently
normalized or truncated Hamiltonian. A standalone matrix-adjoint expression
for the right side is not separately constructed in this module; publication
wording should name the proved quadratic-form identity.

PairOccupationAnnihilation proves
projectionPairs_eq_pairAnnihilate_normSq, namely
<psi,sum_{i<j}P_beta,i P_beta,j psi>=||B_beta psi||^2
on symmetric N+2-particle inputs, with the actual pairAnnihilate definition.
The same exact unordered-pair count provides the required combinatorial
coefficient. This identity remains valid for an unnormalized orbital beta
because both sides scale quartically in beta. Normalization is only needed
for the additional interpretation of P_beta,i as a projector and of this
pair form as n_beta(n_beta-I)/2. The final occupation module supplies that
normalization through its explicit unit spinor hypothesis.

Thus the literal annihilation-norm forms of the intended bounds are supported:
||a_beta psi||^2 <= <psi,H psi>+||psi||^2,
||B_beta psi||^2 <= <psi,H psi>,
and, on the correctly typed post-annihilation sector,
||a_beta B_alpha psi||^2 <= <B_alpha psi,H B_alpha psi>+||B_alpha psi||^2.
The vacuum and one-particle pair-zero cases remain separate trivial sectors;
the explicit double-annihilation maps use the total input index N+2 and do
not pretend to remove particles from an insufficiently populated sector.
