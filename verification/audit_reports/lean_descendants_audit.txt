Scope and checked proof structure

1. TensorSlotLinear.lean lifts an arbitrary single-orbital complex linear
   map to one ordered tensor slot. Composition within a slot and commutation
   between distinct slots are derived using the actual configuration-update
   map. The existing slot/spectator bijection proves the physical adjoint
   identity. Summing slots derives the commutator and adjoint relations
   without restricting to bosonic states or substituting an auxiliary space.

2. TensorSpin.lean identifies the existing physical tensorRaise with the
   sum of the normalized single-orbital raising maps. It defines the matching
   total lowering map and the diagonal twice-weight N*Q-2*configurationWeight.
   The actual relations [J+,J-]=2Jz, [2Jz,J-]=-2J-, and J+*=J- are proved.
   Lowering preserves Bose symmetry and sends deficit d to d+1. This layer
   permits all integer Q,N>=0, including zero flux and the vacuum.

3. TensorDescendants.lean defines the physical state J-^n psi using powers
   of that same lowering map. Highest-weight hypotheses are J+ phi=0 and
   2Jz phi=m phi, with natural m. The exact raising recurrence, transferred
   through the physical adjoint identity, proves the sesquilinear factor
   g(m,n)=n! m.descFactorial n for n<=m. The factorial identity and positivity
   are separately proved. The sesquilinear theorem only needs the right
   input to satisfy the highest-weight hypotheses; the left input is arbitrary.
   For the norm and nonzero corollaries both inputs are the same vector.
   Positivity of the physical inner product proves J-^(m+1) psi=0. Normalizing
   by sqrt(g(m,n)) preserves the initial inner product; distinct levels
   are orthogonal for inputs of the same initial deficit. Division is used
   with g(m,n)>0 in the norm-preserving statement.

4. CertificateDescendants.lean transports these facts to the actual normalized
   occupation coordinates using their proved metric and CM-kernel/raising
   equivalence. These instances require Q>0 and 2*d<=N*Q, with m=N*Q-2*d.
   For an arbitrary complete physical highest-weight frame E, it constructs
   the normalized descendant frame and proves its Bose symmetry, support,
   same-level Gram preservation, and cross-level orthogonality. The finite
   indexed statement has n,l in Fin(m+1). It retains all multiplicities and
   does not assume an orthonormal initial frame. The identity-operator form
   matrix equality is proved entrywise from the sesquilinear identity.

Scope limitations

These results provide actual normalized physical descendant frames. Their
identification with the verifier's explicit polynomial descendant arrays
remains open. No SU(2) group integral, Haar measure, or Schur-averaged
comparison formula is proved by this extension. It does not assert that
the descendant frames exhaust the full bosonic space across all highest
weights. Full frozen-row assembly, all-particle certificate-row normal
ordering, interval semantics, Taylor remainders, and the final uniform-gap
target remain open. The earlier combined descendant-and-Schur status flag
remains false because the averaging component is still missing. The
manuscript, PDF, numerical certificates, and v0.1 Release are unchanged.
