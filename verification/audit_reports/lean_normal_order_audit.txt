Conclusion
The reviewed statements implement the stated spherical, LLL-projected V0
pair-projector model with pair coefficient one. No meaning-changing defect
was found in the new operator identities or factorial normalizations.
The complete three-body spectral decomposition and the uniform-gap target
remain unproved. This review covers source semantics; the separate build
and axiom audit records establish kernel acceptance for their exact sources.

1. Pair support and normal ordering
normalThreeBodyApply is defined independently as the sum of P_ij P_kl with
i<j, k<l, unequal pairs and at least one common particle slot. Every such
term has exactly three distinct slots. normalFourBodyApply sums the
disjoint case. These are ordered choices of two unordered pairs.
hamiltonian_normal_order proves H(H psi)=H psi+S3 psi+S4 psi on the full
finite tensor space, with no bosonic or spectral hypothesis. The diagonal
term uses the already-proved idempotence of the actual pair projector.
It does not define S3 by subtracting the other sides of the desired identity.
The S3 and S4 Hermitian identities and S4 nonnegativity have the correct
complex inner-product convention. No positivity of S3 is asserted.

2. Disjoint projectors and Fock normalization
FourBody.lean proves actual commutation from disjoint slot updates, then
<psi,P_ij P_kl psi>=||P_ij P_kl psi||^2. FourBodyFock.lean first contracts
the four distinguished slots, then transports them by an actual permutation
using IsBosonic. The count is C(n,2) C(n-2,2), without an extra 1/2: pair
choices remain ordered. This matches the squared normalization of the two
annihilators B_q B_p, whose individual factors are sqrt(C(n,2)) and
sqrt(C(n-2,2)). For four particles the multiplicity is six, as expected.
normalFourBody_eq_sum_v0PairAnnihilate_normSq is a quadratic-form identity
for every bosonic input in State Q (N+4). It is not a separate construction
of operator adjoints on a direct-sum Fock Hilbert space.
normal_order_annihilator_energy and
normalFourBody_eq_sum_v0PairAnnihilate_energy_all_sectors cover every input
State Q (N+2), including two and three particles, where the output H has
zero or one particle. normalFourBody_small_sector covers all N<=3 directly.
Thus small sectors are not silently dropped from the support identity.
coherent_occupation_v0_sum_lift sums the previously proved coherent-mode
occupation bound over physical V0 channels. Its S4+H right-hand side is
identified by the exact annihilator energy and norm identities; it is not
introduced as an unproved tail-error assumption.

3. Concrete three-body maps
ThreeBodyAux Q has coordinates (p,k), with p in Fin(2Q+1) and k in Fin(Q+1).
threeBodyInsertion uses the same normalized pairVector as the Hamiltonian.
threeBodyMap is 1/sqrt(3) times the three distinct pair insertions, hence
sqrt(3) times full bosonic symmetrization of the pair-first insertion.
Its output is proved bosonic. The contraction called threeBodyAdjoint has
factor sqrt(3). threeBody_adjoint_inner proves the actual adjoint identity
when the physical argument is bosonic. This restriction is necessary:
the displayed contraction is not claimed to be the adjoint on arbitrary
unsymmetrized tensor states. threeBody_hamiltonian likewise assumes
IsBosonic and proves WW*=H3 in exactly that sector.
compressedSwap is explicitly the pair-overlap contraction for exchanging
the second pair slot and the spectator. The auxiliary identity
threeBody_gram proves W*W=I+2T for every auxiliary vector. No hypothesized
eigenvalue or SU(2) decomposition is used.

4. Three-body normal-order coefficient
threeBody_normal_coefficient proves S3 psi=2 W T W* psi for State Q 3 with
IsBosonic psi. The derivation combines the already-defined support S3,
the exact H3 square identity, the Gram identity and WW*=H3. It currently
establishes the physical three-particle coefficient, not the general
normal-ordered positive three-body lift on arbitrary particle sectors.

5. Recoupling coefficient and deficit-one scope
recouplingRatio is the descending-factorial ratio (Q)_z/(2Q)_z. The
positivity and 2^(-z) bound assume z<=Q. The z=1 value needs Q>0; Q=0 is
not accidentally divided by zero. The endpoint pairCoefficient(Q,z,0)
is connected to sqrt(recouplingRatio) using the actual binomial pair basis.
highestWeightCoefficient is the finite product of the manuscript's
recurrence factors, with s0=1. Nonzero coefficients, interior ladder
cancellation, and sz=(-1)^z sqrt((Q)_z/(2Q)_z) are proved. These facts do
not by themselves prove that T preserves each highest-weight line or
that the resulting spin multiplets span the auxiliary space.
There is also a genuine operator result: for Q>0 the explicit vector
phi=|p=0,k=1>-sqrt(1/2)|p=1,k=0> is nonzero, W phi=0, and T phi=-phi/2.
This vector is fixed by coordinates; it is not assumed to be an eigenvector.
The proof does not claim to identify the whole z=1 irreducible multiplet.

6. Unresolved proof obligations and trust scope
The full formula T=sum_z (-1)^z f_z Pi_z still needs a formal SU(2) action,
equivariance, construction of the irreducible multiplets and completeness.
The general three-body lifting bridge, finite block certificates,
coherent-tail estimates and final uniform-gap assembly remain separate
obligations. UniformGapTarget in Model.lean remains a proposition definition,
not a proved theorem. No new sorry, axiom, unsafe or native_decide was found
in the reviewed source files. A source scan is not a substitute for the
whole-project #print axioms audit; use its separate verification record.
