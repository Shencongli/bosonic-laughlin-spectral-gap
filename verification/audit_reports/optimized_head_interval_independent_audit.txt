COORDINATES AND DIRECT NORMAL ORDERING

Put P_j=product_{l<j}(1-l x), E_p=product_{l<p}(1-l x/2), d_j=sqrt(P_j),
e_p=sqrt(E_p), x=1/Q. Affine canonical polynomial coordinates represent the
physical sphere vector D_A^-1|A]_x. Their metric is g_A/product_{j in A}P_j,
exactly Model.metric. In these coordinates B_p=(kappa_p/e_p)Bhat_p and
a_j=(sqrt(j!)/d_j) derivative_j. With the frozen definitions
y0=lambda*kappa_t and y_pj=alpha_pj*kappa_p*sqrt(j!/i!), i=p+j-t,
the physical three-body cross term has form coefficient

  y0*y_pj*i!/(e_t e_p d_i d_j)

between complete annihilation rows Bhat_t derivative_i and Bhat_p derivative_j.
This agrees with h*ei[t] and both Hermitian cross terms in make_body_forms.
The quadratic commutator term has i=i' and coefficient

  y_pj*y_p'j'*i!/(e_p e_p' d_j d_j').

It correctly has no d_i^-2 factor, because the physical commutator is delta_ii'.
The four-body term has coefficient

  y_pj*y_p'j'*i!i'!/(e_p e_p' d_i d_j d_i' d_j'),

between rows Bhat_p derivative_j derivative_i' and
Bhat_p' derivative_i derivative_j'. This is exactly h*h'; the common weight
is p+j+i'=p+j+p'+j'-t. The ordered pair loop and E.ann supply the correct
canonical occupation factors, including repeated indices. C[a][b] is the
exact rational row Gram sum. These formulas were independently rederived.

HAMILTONIAN FORMS, DESCENDANTS, AND FIXED KERNEL

Hform=sum(kappa_p^2/E_p)Bhat_p^T Gout Bhat_p, hence the physical H^2 form is
Hform G^-1 Hform, as implemented. S4 uses the ordered p,q=M-p sum with
kappa_p^2 kappa_q^2/(E_p E_q) and vacuum metric one. The affine coordinate
representation of J_-/sqrt(Q) has the planar canonical raising coefficient
times (1-j/Q), not its square root. raise_frame is correct, and its kth
squared normalization k[r-(2z+k-1)/Q] and Schur factor
(2+x)/(r+(1-2z)x) are correct.

The affine B identity fixes exactly the same highest-space H kernel for
all Q. Normal-ordered K3,K4 and both targets kill that kernel. active_frame
selects a positive principal minor of the exact planar H form, hence a
complement of its kernel; orthogonality is unnecessary since the forms kill
all cross terms to the kernel. Decimal Cholesky only chooses a rational
conditioning matrix; its triangular shape and exact nonzero diagonal prove
invertibility. No accuracy of that floating choice is needed for positivity.
All later entries use those rational values with rigorous interval enclosure.
A rank-zero block is identically zero by common-kernel annihilation.

DIRECTED INTERVAL ARITHMETIC

I stores closed integer endpoints in units 2^-192. The four endpoint
products and outward floor/ceil divisions correctly enclose multiplication,
including both signs. inv gives floor(U^2/hi),ceil(U^2/lo), valid for positive
or negative intervals avoiding zero. sqrt uses floor/ceiling integer square
roots. Negative square-root domains or zero-containing inverses fail
explicitly. Floats occur only in reports, apart from the rational basis
choice just discussed; they do not decide any positivity inequality.

TAYLOR-JET INVARIANT AND REMAINDERS

The invariant is f(x)=sum_{i=0}^O a_i x^i+x^(O+1)r_f(x), with every true
Taylor coefficient and remainder enclosed by its interval. Product high
convolution terms supply sum_{k>O}c_k x^(k-O-1); the cross Horner loop is
exactly P_f r_g+r_f P_g+x^(O+1)r_f r_g. The range method includes the full
remainder term. For inv, the formal Taylor recurrence makes the TRUE low
coefficients of f P_b-1 vanish exactly, so the inverse remainder is
-(f P_b-1)_remainder/f. For sqrt, the recurrence similarly cancels the low
coefficients of f-P_c^2 and uses

  sqrt(f)-P_c=(f-P_c^2)/(sqrt(f)+P_c).

Both are precisely the implemented formulas. Interval low coefficients
need not numerically equal zero: exact algebraic cancellation applies to
the enclosed true coefficients, while interval arithmetic overestimates
their high-order remainder. This is not an assumed floating cancellation.

BERNSTEIN CERTIFICATION

After symmetrization, the true remainder matrix is symmetric and its norm
is bounded by the maximum absolute row sum of interval endpoints. Multiplying
by Qmin^-(O+1) and rounding upward gives certify's error. For t=Qmin*x,
the Bernstein control b is sum_{a<=b} A_a binom(b,a)/[binom(O,a)Qmin^a],
exactly as implemented. Subtracting error*I from every control subtracts
that constant matrix polynomial. Positive interval LDL pivots certify every
actual symmetric matrix enclosed, and Bernstein convexity then gives the
matrix inequality on the entire interval. The de Casteljau midpoint scheme,
left/right extraction, and finite-depth failure handling are correct.

COVERAGE

The code requires Qmin>=1024, ensuring the retained spaces and ladder formulas
are valid. --body/--z correctly mark selected-block output; a complete claim
requires all 26 blocks. Changing shifts requires a new successful certificate
and the corresponding scalar budget. The interval proof covers all real x
