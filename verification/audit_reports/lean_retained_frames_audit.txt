Sparse integer checker
----------------------
SparseIntRow n is a finite list of (Fin n, integer) entries. Its mathematical
interpretation sums all entries with a given column. Repeated column indices,
unsorted inputs and explicit zero entries are therefore allowed. sparseIntInsert
updates the first matching entry and may delete it when its sum is zero; the
remaining entries are retained. The proved evaluation identity remains correct
even if later entries have the same column. Row addition, scaling and product are
proved against the full integer matrix operations by induction on the stored
lists. No sortedness or uniqueness assumption is hidden in those theorems.

SparseScaledFrameCheck requires literal sparse-row results [] for CW, singleton
[(i,D)] for LW, and singleton [(i,D)] for WL+ZC. These conditions are sufficient,
not claimed necessary. sparseScaledFrameCheck_integer correctly converts them to
CW=0, LW=D I, WL+ZC=D I as integer matrix identities, including empty dimensions.

sparseScaledFrameCheck_sound assumes the explicit condition D != 0. Its image
in Q is nonzero. With U=D^{-1}W and V=D^{-1}Z, multiplying the integer identities
by D^{-1} proves C U=0, L U=I, U L+V C=I. These are all three HasKernelFrame
identities. In particular the third identity supplies completeness of the kernel,
not only independence of the proposed columns. L U=I supplies injectivity.
No numerical rank or floating-point tolerance enters this argument.

Degree-zero boundary
--------------------
The script has target parts(N,-1)=empty at d=0, so C is a 0-by-1 matrix. Lean's
natural subtraction makes the nominal occupationCMBlock target degree 0; its
target is one-dimensional and the map is zero. There is no row-index equivalence
between those targets. I raised this point before physical transport. The
RetainedCMZeroDegree.lean implementation treats it separately: it proves the
unique source occupation, actual CM map zero, a full physical highest-weight
linear equivalence, dimension one, and that each retained degree-zero U is [1].
It proves completeness of those actual columns without inventing a row bijection.
These coordinate maps are not asserted to be orthonormal.

Physical transport (self-review)
--------------------------------
KernelFramePhysical composes the complex frame equivalence, the inverse index
transport, the exact equality between CM matrix multiplication and the CM linear
map, and occupationHighestWeightEquiv. Its actual physical map is D_Q^{-1} after
the factorial occupation inclusion. It gives a linear equivalence with the full
physicalHighestWeightSubspace and its dimension. The scalar-normalized certificate

identified with those normalized coordinates.
Matrix identification with the actual CM operator is an explicit premise
of the generic interface. The generated enumeration and entry theorems discharge
that premise in all 24 retained positive-degree sectors.

Complete enumeration and actual CM entries
-----------------------------------------
PartitionsEnumeration proves membership in degreePartitions N d lo is equivalent
to length N, total sum d, nondecreasing entries, and lower bound lo. Its recursion
restricts the first entry to 0,...,d and the remaining entries to values at least
that first entry. The proved no-duplicate property and the get/ofFn inverse
identities provide both injectivity and surjectivity. EnumeratedPartitions turns
an exact table equality into a SortedTupleEnumeration. It does not postulate the
completeness of the Python list.

SortedCMCoefficient relates each computable natural-number entry to the actual
occupation CM matrix. A target row B is connected to a source A by replacing an
entry i of B by i+1; its coefficient is (i+1) count_A(i+1). This is exactly the
inverse description of lowering one occupied source orbital j with coefficient
j n_j. The counting factor refers to the source A. The conversion from bounded
orbitals to natural labels is injective, preserving both counts and replacement.
For positive degree d and Q >= d, the finite sum truncates to i<d by the target
degree d-1. The source and target maps use their correct respective degrees.

IndexedOccupationCM introduces a genuine bijective enumeration on both sides.
Its matrix-entry equality is then applied by RetainedCMCoordinates: all 26 literal
index tables have kernel-checked equality to the complete recursive enumeration;
all 24 positive-degree integer entry arrays have kernel-checked equality to the
tuple coefficient. Each resulting physical_matrix theorem is unconditional apart
from the displayed bound Q >= d. There is no Python-success premise or hidden
CM-entry-identification hypothesis in those concrete theorems.

RetainedCMPhysical composes these concrete identifications with each complete
rational frame. The 24 physicalEquiv declarations are linear equivalences onto
the full actual spherical bosonic highest-weight subspaces. Each physical_apply
statement identifies its vector explicitly as D_Q^{-1} of the factorial occupation
embedding of (U cast to complex) times the coefficient vector, with the inverse
source enumeration. Each columns_complete statement concerns this actual map,
and the dimensions are its complex dimensions. The two degree-zero dimensions
use the separately proved zero-map construction described above. Empty highest-
weight spaces, including d=1, are covered by Fin 0 coefficient spaces.
