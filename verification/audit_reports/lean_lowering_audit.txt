Scope and checked proof structure

1. PolynomialLowering.lean derives the actual total J- map in polynomial
   coordinates from the binomial-root ladder identity. The output orbital
   i>0 has source i-1 and coefficient Q-(i-1). All finite-cap boundary
   cases are included. The polynomial map is conjugate to the actual
   N-particle tensor lowering operator, not an auxiliary replacement.

2. OccupationLowering.lean passes through the factorial occupation inclusion.
   Its multiplicity is that of the removed source label, proved using the
   occupation-factorial identity. Deficit d maps to d+1. Defining R=Q^{-1}L
   gives J- Phi_d=sqrt(Q) Phi_(d+1) R for Q>0. The coefficient includes the
   degree-dependent normalization of Phi. The matrix entry reconstructs the
   source by output i -> i-1, equivalent to source j -> j+1.

3. IndexedOccupationLowering.lean gives computable multiset formulas for
   the constant and slope coefficients. Their support permits restricting
   the sum to output labels at most d+1. Under Q>=d+1, the previously proved
   sorted enumerations identify the complete physical occupation indices
   in the verifier's actual order. The matrix equals C-Q^{-1}S and its
   multiplication is proved to act as the physical coordinate map on
   arbitrary coefficient columns.

4. RetainedLoweringArrays.lean checks every integer entry of the 24 pairs
   C,S by direct Lean kernel reduction. There are 8 steps for N=3, d=0..7,
   and 16 for N=4, d=0..15. The last matrix is 64 by 54. The physical
   equality is quantified over all integer Q>=d+1, not sampled Q values.
   The generated definitions retain the existing complete partition tables.

5. VerifierDescendants.lean iterates R with dependent degree labels and
   proves J-^k Phi_d=Q^(k/2) Phi_(d+k) V_k for all input columns at Q>0.
   For m=N*Q-2*d>=0 and k<=m, the exact verifier recurrence
   rho_(k+1)=rho_k*(k+1)*(N-(2*d+k)/Q) equals g(m,k)/Q^k and is positive.
   On the CM kernel, the descendant Gram form is rho_k times the initial
   metric. For an arbitrary physical linear operator A, the normalized
   descendant form equals rho_k^{-1} times the form pulled back through
   Phi_(d+k) V_k. That scalar form identity does not require A to be
   Hermitian, rotationally invariant, or positive. Initial-metric
   preservation uses the explicit highest-weight condition.
