1. Bosonic projection and tensor slices
---------------------------------------
The actual projection is
  P_M psi(a) = (M!)^(-1) sum_sigma psi(a composed with sigma),
where sigma runs over all permutations of the M particle slots. The factor
is the actual permutation-group order, including the singleton M=0 group.
The proofs establish linearity, a bosonic image, identity on bosonic inputs,
idempotence, and self-adjointness for the coordinate inner product.

ThreeBodySlices splits a configuration into its first three slots and the
remaining N slots by an explicitly inverted finite equivalence. Consequently
the inner product is exactly the sum over spectator configurations of the
three-slot inner products, with no multiplicity or factorial in that step.
Slicing a bosonic M-particle state is proved to give a bosonic three-particle
state, by embedding each three-slot permutation into the M-slot permutation
group. Pair replacement and pair-projector actions are proved compatible
with the same insertion/slice maps. A slice need not be normalized; the
subsequent statements are homogeneous sesquilinear or quadratic identities.

2. Lift normalization and positivity
------------------------------------
For M=N+3 the independently defined lift is
  L_3^M(A) = choose(M,3) P_M (A tensor I_N) P_M.
Here the tensor action is the actual firstThreeApply on the first three
coordinate slots, and both P_M factors are explicit. The right factor
makes all slices of an arbitrary ambient input physical three-boson states;
the left factor guarantees a bosonic output. On bosonic input the right
factor is the identity. For M=0,1,2 the lift is explicitly zero.

The sesquilinear form is proved to be choose(M,3) times the sum over
spectators of the physical three-particle forms. Thus nonnegative real
quadratic forms and their ordering are preserved, requiring the hypothesis
only on physical three-boson vectors. No hypothesis of positivity on the
larger unphysical tensor space is inserted.

The generic nonnegativity theorem alone concerns Re<chi,A chi>; this is not
by itself a self-adjointness statement for arbitrary A. Following this
review observation, ThreeBodyLiftHermitian separately proves that physical
self-adjointness of A is preserved as full ambient self-adjointness of the
lift, for every M including M<3. Together these are genuine preservation
of positive physical operators. The spectral coefficients use actual
Hermitian orthogonal auxiliary projectors, not only an assumed real-part
inequality.

The identity coefficient obeys L_3^M(I) = choose(M,3) I on the physical
sector, confirming the chosen many-body normalization.

3. Identification with the actual normal-order three-body term
-------------------------------------------------------------
normalThreeBodyApply is the pre-existing independent sum of ordered products
of two unequal overlapping unordered pair projectors. It is not redefined
as a lift or as H^2-H-S4 during this increment.

For each fixed unordered pair there are exactly 2(M-2) unequal unordered
pairs sharing one slot. Therefore the number of ordered overlapping-pair
products is
  choose(M,2) * 2(M-2) = 6 choose(M,3).
The factor six counts the six ordered choices of two distinct edges of a
fixed three-slot triangle. The same six products occur in the actual
three-particle normal-order coefficient. This cancels the six on comparing
the many-body and three-body forms, leaving the required choose(M,3).

ThreeBodyCounting reduces each complex sesquilinear matrix element between
two bosonic vectors to the first shared-slot product P_01 P_02, preserving
operator order. It does not assume overlapping projectors commute. The
reduction uses an explicitly extended permutation of three distinct slots
and the proven pair-exchange symmetry.

ThreeBodySymmetry independently proves that normalThreeBodyApply preserves
the bosonic sector. Its symmetric overlap predicate agrees with the
original predicate when both pairs are in increasing order. Reindexing
the symmetric unordered-pair sums proves preservation without assuming
the desired lift identity. ThreeBodyLiftIdentity then uses the independent
counting identity, the exact slice identity, and bosonic inner-product
extensionality to prove equality of actual operators on bosonic inputs.
This is stronger than equality of diagonal expectation values or a statement
only about P_M S3 P_M. For M<3 the original S3 sum is separately proved zero.
No circular dependence on the lift identity was found.

4. Creation, annihilation, and rank-one normalization
----------------------------------------------------
The three-particle annihilator is the actual contraction
  A(w) psi(a) = sqrt(choose(M,3)) <w, slice_a(P_M psi)>.
Its creation partner is the explicitly symmetrized tensor insertion with
the same square-root coefficient. The source proves their sesquilinear
adjoint relation, that both relevant outputs are bosonic, and
  A(w)^dagger |vac> = w
for bosonic w and the normalized vacuum coordinate. No unit-norm assumption
on w is required for this linear identity.

The rank-one coefficient is literally |w><w'|, acting as
  chi -> w <w',chi>.
The rank-one lifting identity is proved as composition of the defined
creation and annihilation maps, and its two square roots multiply to
choose(M,3). There is no extra 3! in this convention. This verifies the
operational normal-order rule in finite normalized symmetric tensors.
It does not additionally formalize an occupation-monomial expansion of
every A(w) in the one-particle CCR operators; no such stronger claim is
needed for, or made by, the lifting identity.

5. Auxiliary coefficients and the all-particle spectral formula
---------------------------------------------------------------
The physical coefficient is W_3 A W_3^dagger, with the already established
sqrt(3) normalization of W_3. threeBodyAdjoint is only used as the actual
adjoint against a bosonic physical bra, exactly where its earlier adjoint
identity applies. This is not silently extended to an unrestricted
ambient-adjoint identity.

For each previously verified auxiliary spectral projector Pi_z, the
physical coefficient W_3 Pi_z W_3^dagger is nonnegative on the physical
three-boson sector. The auxiliary nonnegativity is derived from the actual
matrix's Hermitian and idempotence theorems. Its physical Hermitian property
is supplied separately. Lifting these coefficients yields nonnegative
quadratic forms on all ambient finite particle sectors because of P_M on
both sides of the lift.

The final S3 decomposition retains the signed coefficients
  2 (-1)^z (Q descending z)/(2Q descending z).
The positivity of each lifted block does not make S3 positive, and the
source does not make that inference. The stated S3 and H^2 decompositions
are exact on bosonic inputs for every finite Q and particle number. They
do not introduce or prove a uniform gap threshold, complete the remaining
finite certificates or tail estimates, or change the full-gap theorem's
unproved status.
