Scope and checked proof structure

1. CertificatePairIntertwiner.lean derives the exact coefficient sqrt(kappa_p^2 /
   P_p(2Q)) in the physical pair-annihilation coordinate map. At integer Q>0,
   p ranges over Fin(2Q+1). The weight-sector identity covers p above the input
   degree, when both sides vanish. Composing two maps retains each intermediate
   degree and its normalization.

2. CertificateFourBody.lean defines the integer double-pair matrix and its
   output-metric Gram form. It proves the physical double-annihilator
   sesquilinear identity and the actual S4 quadratic-form identity on every
   N+4-particle sector, Q>0. The matrix is positive semidefinite. The S4 theorem
   is stated as a quadratic-form equality, not a separate complex-entry
   operator-matrix equality.

3. CertificateSingleIntertwiner.lean proves the sqrt(j! / P_j(Q)) factor for
   single-orbital annihilation, j<=Q, with j<=d in its degree-labelled map.
   Single-pair and double-single-pair compositions track the remaining degree.
   These maps act on actual occupation coordinates in the ordered-tensor model.

4. LowSectorTransfer.lean derives the one- and two-particle coordinate inner
   products and the transfer-operator contractions from physical definitions.
   Two-particle transfer identities require bosonic inputs; the swap symmetry,
   sqrt(2) normalization, and quartic contraction factor are proved.

5. CertificateRowNormalOrder.lean defines a finite selected row with arbitrary
   complex coefficients. It agrees with certificateRow when all index triples
   are selected and annihilates the actual Hamiltonian kernel at arbitrary
   particle number. The normal-order equalities are proved for three- and
   four-particle input sectors; the latter requires bosonic inputs. Their
   nonnegative sums are actual row norms. The arbitrary-particle-number
   normal-order equality for these selected rows remains unproved.

6. CertificateVacuumRows.lean identifies the integer-derivative functionals
   for one and two spectator annihilators, including repeated labels. The
   input degree equals the sum of pair and spectator labels. Exact vacuum
   normalization gives the physical coefficients and cross inner products.

7. CertificateRowFactors.lean uses the specified real frozen-row conventions,
   with unrestricted signs. Exact scalar cancellations give the direct K3
   cross/contraction and K4 factors. This is algebra at finite Q, not a
   Taylor expansion or an interval estimate.

8. CertificateDirectTerms.lean combines these scalar cancellations with the
   physical vacuum-functional identities. Its three theorems identify the
   direct terms at matching degrees, for arbitrary real row entries, Q>0,
   pair indices at most 2Q, and orbital indices at most Q.
