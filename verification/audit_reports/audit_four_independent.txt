1. EXACT MAP AND FOCK NORMALIZATIONS

Let U_Q be spin Q/2 and V_Q the spin-Q subspace of Sym^2 U_Q. On physical
four-boson space, B_p maps Sym^4 U_Q into Sym^2 U_Q. Therefore the adjoint
of W(v_p tensor u)=B_p^dagger u is

  W^dagger psi = sum_p v_p tensor (B_p psi),
  W W^dagger = sum_p B_p^dagger B_p = H4,Q.

This identity is exact. W is neither an isometry nor required to be
injective. An independent first-quantized check is
W=sqrt(6) P_sym^(4) J, with J including a V_Q pair and a symmetric
spectator pair into four tensor slots. For four particles in e_0,
W(v_0 tensor |2 e_0>)=sqrt(6)|4 e_0>, matching H4 eigenvalue 6.

The canonical symmetric-tensor/Fock isometry sends

  P_sym^(2)(beta tensor gamma)
       to a_beta^dagger a_gamma^dagger|vac>/sqrt(2).

Both sides have norm squared (1+|<beta,gamma>|^2)/2. Thus this statement
does not silently assume orthogonal orbitals, and beta=gamma gives a
normalized vector. After W, the creation polynomial of this possibly
unnormalized four-particle vector is

  B_alpha^dagger a_beta^dagger a_gamma^dagger / sqrt(2).

Consequently its rank-one four-body lift is exactly

  (1/2) B_alpha^dagger A^dagger A B_alpha,
  A=a_gamma a_beta.

No binomial or additional spectator factor belongs in this expression.

Equivariance implies Pi_aux W^dagger=W^dagger Pi_phys when both Pi's
retain the complete same total-spin isotypic components, hence

  W Pi_aux W^dagger = H4,Q Pi_phys.

Retaining ALL multiplicities is essential to this particular argument.

2. EXACT NONORTHOGONAL OCCUPATION ESTIMATE

Put a=a_beta, b=a_gamma, s=[a,b^dagger]=<beta,gamma> and n_a=a^dagger a,
n_b=b^dagger b. CCR give

  n_a n_b=A^dagger A+s a^dagger b,
  n_b n_a=A^dagger A+conjugate(s) b^dagger a.

Thus the displayed Hermitian-part identity in the source is correct.
The positive square (n_a-n_b)^2 gives

  (n_a n_b+n_b n_a)/2 <= (n_a^2+n_b^2)/2.

The positive squares (a +/- exp(i phi)b)^dagger(a +/- exp(i phi)b)
give -Re(s a^dagger b) <= |s|(n_a+n_b)/2 <= (n_a+n_b)/2. Neither
step assumes [n_a,n_b]=0.

For a COHERENT orbital x, its pair vector lies exactly in V_Q, so

  H_Q >= B_x^dagger B_x = n_x(n_x-1)/2.

This is a pair-channel Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, not a many-body gap.
The scalar integer inequality n <= n(n-1)/2+1, n=0,1,..., gives
n_x<=H_Q+I. Also n_x^2<=2H_Q+n_x. Combining,

  A^dagger A
    <= (n_beta^2+n_gamma^2)/2+(n_beta+n_gamma)/2
    <= 2H_Q+n_beta+n_gamma <=4H_Q+2I.

Here H_Q acts on the N-2 sector when this is inserted between B_alpha
and B_alpha^dagger. This bookkeeping is valid for every N.

3. EXACT COHERENT MARGINAL AND POSITIVE LIFT

Use unit-mass spherical measures and d=Q+1, da=2Q+1. The scalar overlap
s(alpha,beta)=cos^2(theta/2) is uniformly distributed on [0,1] at fixed
alpha, hence each spectator marginal is d/(M+1). The exact pair frame is

  da integral B_alpha^dagger X B_alpha dOmega_alpha
       =sum_p B_p^dagger X B_p

for any fixed operator X on the intermediate sector. Setting X=I gives
H_Q; setting X=H_Q gives S4,Q, since pair annihilators commute. Therefore

  L4(W A_Q W^dagger)
       <= [d/(M+1)]^2 (2S4,Q+H_Q).                    (A)

All steps in (A) are exact at finite Q. Positivity of L4 is enough:
a positive few-body matrix decomposes into positive rank-one terms,
each lifted to A(w)^dagger A(w). An isometric lift is not required.

4. EXACT SCHUR FORMULA AND STABLE MULTIPLICITIES

At the north pole define

  R_Q=d integral s(north,beta)^M |beta><beta| dOmega_beta.

Its eigenvalues in normalized single-particle orbital j are

  b_j,Q = (Q+1)/(M+Q+1) * (Q)_j/(M+Q)_j, 0<=j<=Q.

This follows by integrating binomial(Q,j)s^(M+Q-j)(1-s)^j with the
beta-function factorial formula. The north-pole seed is

  T_Q=|v_0><v_0| tensor (R_Q tensor R_Q)|Sym^2 U_Q.

It is invariant under rotations fixing the north pole, so the original
sphere integral is EXACTLY da times its normalized SU(2) conjugation
average. Schur averaging on the spin-J isotypic component gives identity
on that spin representation times the multiplicity matrix

  (A_z,Q)_{bc} = da/(2J+1) sum_{n=0}^{2J}
      <chi_b,n,Q|T_Q|chi_c,n,Q>,  J=2Q-z.             (B)

The seed has pair orbital p=0. At descendant n the total weight deficit
is z+n, so (B) is precisely the stated sum over j<=k, j+k=z+n. In the
orthonormal spectator basis, |j,k> has denominator sqrt(2) for j<k,
while |j,j>=e_j tensor e_j. In BOTH cases the seed eigenvalue is b_j b_k;
the sum must NOT carry a further factor 2 or 1/2.

For Q>=z, the weight-z auxiliary basis is indexed by p+j+k=z with
p>=0 and 0<=j<=k. Its dimension f(z) is independent of Q, with generating
function 1/[(1-t)^2(1-t^2)]. The multiplicity at J=2Q-z is

  mu_z=f(z)-f(z-1)=floor(z/2)+1.                      (C)

One can justify stable highest-weight bases constructively. Rescale
spherical J_+ by sqrt(Q). On these finite weight spaces it converges to

  sqrt(2) a_u+a_v+a_w = 2 a_eta,
  eta=(sqrt(2)u+v+w)/2.

The limiting annihilator is onto the degree-(z-1) symmetric spectator
space: (1/2)a_eta^dagger(N_eta+1)^(-1) is a right inverse of 2a_eta.
Thus the finite matrices have full row rank for sufficiently large Q.
Their kernel projectors

  I-T_z,Q^dagger(T_z,Q T_z,Q^dagger)^(-1)T_z,Q

converge in norm. Project a fixed limiting orthonormal kernel basis and
multiply by the inverse square root of its Gram matrix. This constructs
convergent orthonormal finite-Q highest-weight bases explicitly.

The limiting relative coordinates are

  xi_1=(v-w)/sqrt(2),
  xi_2=u/sqrt(2)-(v+w)/2.

The three coordinate rows (eta,xi_1,xi_2) are orthonormal. Spectator
exchange changes only xi_1's sign. A complete limiting highest basis is

  (a_xi1^dagger)^(2k) (a_xi2^dagger)^(z-2k)|vac>
       /sqrt[(2k)!(z-2k)!], 0<=k<=floor(z/2).

This independently recovers (C) and includes the n1=0 channel needed for
the claimed minimum eigenvalue.

For fixed n, the exact descendant normalization is
sqrt[n!(2J)_n]. Since J_-/sqrt(Q) tends to 2a_eta^dagger, the normalized
descendants tend to (a_eta^dagger)^n/sqrt(n!) times the highest vector.

5. DOMINATED CONVERGENCE OF THE ENTIRE DESCENDANT SUM

Fix a>0, M=floor(aQ). For Q>=2/a, M>=aQ/2. Every falling-factorial ratio
is bounded by [Q/(Q+M)]^j, and the leading factor is <=1. Therefore

  0<=b_j,Q<=(1+a/2)^(-j)=q^j, q<1.

At fixed descendant n, normalized vectors satisfy sum_{j,k}|c_b,jk|^2
<=1 on the pair-highest slice. Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the absolute matrix
entry of the inner sum in (B) by q^(z+n). The dimension ratio is <=2 for
Q>=z. Extend all nonexistent descendants and orbitals by zero. The
whole summand is bounded by 2q^(z+n), summable over all n independently
of Q. Each fixed-n finite-weight coefficient converges, and
b_j,Q -> r^(j+1), r=(1+a)^(-1). Thus ordinary scalar dominated
convergence, entry by entry in the fixed finite multiplicity dimension,
proves matrix-norm convergence of the FULL sum. This does not exchange
an uncontrolled number of descendants with the Q limit.

6. THE REQUIRED BRIDGE FROM SCHUR LIMIT TO THE PLANAR GAUSSIAN

In three oscillator factors (u,v,w), restricted to spectator exchange
symmetry, define the positive trace-class seed

  T_inf=|0_u><0_u| tensor r^(N_v+1) tensor r^(N_w+1).

Equation (B), the factor da/(2J+1)->1/2, and the descendant convergence
just proved show that the limiting relative multiplicity matrix is the
degree-z block of

  (1/2) Tr_eta(T_inf).                               (D)

This is an operator on the relative oscillators, not on physical
four-boson space. The partial trace sums ALL normalized eta descendants.

Here is an exact way to identify (D). Simultaneously displace the three
oscillators by (sqrt(2)alpha,alpha,alpha). This displaces eta by 2alpha
and leaves xi_1,xi_2 fixed. The displacement twirl identity is

  integral d^2 t/pi D_eta(t) T D_eta(t)^dagger
       = I_eta tensor Tr_eta T.

For completeness, with t=(q+ip)/sqrt(2), its measure is dq dp/(2pi).
The position-space kernel is multiplied by exp[ip(x-y)] and translated
from (x,y) to (x-q,y-q). Integrating p yields delta(x-y), and integrating
q traces the CM kernel. This proves the identity weakly for positive
trace-class T, including the operator-valued relative kernel here.

Since t=2alpha,

  integral 2d^2 alpha/pi D_sim(alpha)T_inf D_sim(alpha)^dagger
       = (1/2) I_eta tensor Tr_eta T_inf.              (E)

The factor 1/2 in (E) is ESSENTIAL: 2d^2 alpha=(1/2)d^2 t. It agrees
exactly with the large-Q Schur dimension ratio, so no normalization
constant is inferred heuristically from local sphere flattening.

Resolve each r^(N+1) as

  integral d^2 beta/pi exp(-a|beta|^2)|beta><beta|.

After the simultaneous displacement, (E) becomes the triple coherent
integral with measure 2d^2 alpha d^2 beta d^2 gamma/pi^3 and weight
exp[-a(|beta-alpha|^2+|gamma-alpha|^2)]. Put u=sqrt(2)alpha, v=beta,
w=gamma. The measure is now d^2u d^2v d^2w/pi^3; the unitary Jacobi
change has Jacobian one; and

  |beta-alpha|^2+|gamma-alpha|^2=|xi_1|^2+2|xi_2|^2.

The one-oscillator Gaussian diagonal matrix element is exactly

  integral d^2 xi/pi e^(-c|xi|^2) |<n|xi>|^2
       =1/(1+c)^(n+1).

Consequently (E) equals

  I_eta tensor r1^(N_1+1) r2^(N_2+1),
  r1=(1+a)^(-1), r2=(1+2a)^(-1),

on the even-N_1 spectator-symmetric subspace. This proves the Gaussian
identification asserted in the draft, rather than assuming it.

A separate normalization check: at z=0 the limiting descendant slice
has total squared pair-highest amplitude (1/2)^n. Equation (B) therefore
gives (1/2)sum_n r^(n+2)2^(-n)=r^2/(2-r)
=1/[(1+a)(1+2a)], exactly r1 r2.

7. LOWER BOUND, LIFT, AND UNIFORMITY IN N

At fixed z, N_1+N_2=z and N_1 is even. Since 0<r2<r1<1, the smallest
eigenvalue is attained at N_1=0 and is r1 r2^(z+1). Across the finite
retained set 0<=z<=D the smallest limiting value is

  t_D=r1 r2^(D+1)>0.

Fixed-block norm convergence therefore implies, for any scalar delta>0
and all sufficiently large Q,

  Pi_aux,<=D <= (t_D^(-1)+delta) A_Q.                (F)

This really is a WHOLE-auxiliary-space inequality: Pi_aux retains full
isotypic components; rotational invariance forbids cross terms between
these components and different-spin components; A_Q is positive on
every unretained component. Invariance alone would NOT justify (F) for
an arbitrary subspace inside a multiplicity block, but that is not the
cutoff being used.

Compose (F) with W and W^dagger, apply positive L4, and use exact (A).
Since d/(floor(aQ)+1)->1/a, for every eps>0 the resulting exact inequality
eventually has coefficient

  C4,D(a)+eps,  C4,D(a)=(1+a)(1+2a)^(D+1)/a^2.

At D=16, a=1/16 this is 272(9/8)^17=2014.47455...<2100.
The threshold only involves a,D,eps and finite auxiliary blocks. All
inequalities involving Fock-space operators are exact after that choice,
so no growing-N norm convergence, N-dependent error, or hidden density
restriction occurs. For N<4 the left side vanishes. Direct-sum form
interpretation then gives the stated all-sector result.
