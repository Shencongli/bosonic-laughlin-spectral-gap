RIGOROUS PARAMETER-INTERVAL HEAD CERTIFICATE
===========================================

Scope and current result
------------------------
optimized_head_interval.py proves E_{r,Q}+delta_{r,z} H_{r,Q}>=0 on each
retained highest block, for EVERY integer Q>=Qmin. The shifts may depend
on r,z and are read as exact rational numbers from a JSON file. The proof
is an enclosure on the entire REAL interval 0<=x=1/Q<=1/Qmin, including
x=0. It does not extrapolate sampled spherical fluxes.

The frozen operator rows are unchanged. There is no premise involving an
unknown many-body gap. Unlike the earlier continuity bound, this new
verifier directly proves the planar and spherical comparison together;
it does not need a separate shifted-planar PSD premise or half-gap premise.

Example already verified, in 14 seconds on the present machine:
  python -S optimized_head_interval.py --qmin 4000
      --shifts optimized_head_shifts_Q4000.json
All 26 blocks pass. At Qmin=4000, these shifts are L_r/Cbar_{r,z}, with
L_3=0.0035, L_4=0.0022, a_3=83/500, a_4=43/500. The Cbar coefficients
and their lifting proof are supplied separately by
optimized_coherent_coefficients.py and optimized_coherent_threshold.txt.

1. Fixed polynomial coordinates, exact metric, and exact common kernel
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the canonical polynomial occupation basis of exact_relative_blocks.py:
|A]_x=product_j x_j^{n_j}|0>, with x_j=sqrt(j!) a_j^dagger and
partial_j=a_j/sqrt(j!). Its planar Gram metric is the positive diagonal
g_A=product_j n_j! (j!)^{n_j}. x_j,partial_j are formal polynomial creation
and differentiation operators, not a physical adjoint pair.

At fixed n,M<=16 define
 P_j(x)=product_{l=0}^{j-1}(1-l x),
 T_p(x)=product_{l=0}^{p-1}(1-l x/2),
 d_j=sqrt(P_j), e_p=sqrt(T_p), d_A=product_{j in A}d_j.
The physical spherical vector associated with a polynomial coordinate
column c is sum_A c_A d_A^{-1}|A]_x, where |A]_x uses the spherical
normalized occupation creators. The metric of these coordinates is

  G_{n,M}(x)=diag(g_A/d_A^2).                                 (1)

The diagonal-map identities from explicit_head_threshold.txt are exact:
J_+/sqrt(Q)=D_{M-1}^{-1} L D_M and
B_{p,Q}=e_p^{-1} D_out^{-1} B_{p,infinity}D_in.
Thus the polynomial highest space is the FIXED rational kernel of
L=sum_{j>=1} j x_{j-1} partial_j. If U is a full rational basis for this
kernel, ker(U^T Hpoly(x) U) is the same coefficient subspace for every x
in the interval, where Hpoly denotes the quadratic form in (1).

Every K_3/K_4 term is a pair sandwich. It and the target forms annihilate
that Hamiltonian kernel exactly, including after SU(2) averaging. Therefore
one may prove positivity on ANY fixed linear complement of this kernel.
The program chooses principal pivot coordinates of the exact planar H
form. Their principal matrix is positive definite by rational elimination.
It then changes basis on this complement by a rational invertible upper
triangular matrix P. P is proposed by Decimal Cholesky, but rounded to exact
rationals and explicitly checked to have nonzero diagonal and zero entries
below the diagonal. Decimal accuracy is irrelevant to validity: P is just
a choice of rational coordinates. It approximately makes H(0)=I and keeps
the rigorous interval bounds well conditioned.

2. Direct normal-ordered body forms
-----------------------------------
Let t be a channel, with row-column labels 0 and a=(p,j), and i=p+j-t.
The frozen rational coefficients are y_{rho,0},y_{rho,a}. Define the exact
rational covariance
  C_ab=sum_rho y_{rho,a} y_{rho,b}
within the channel. Let
  h_a(x)=i!/(e_p d_i d_j).
For n=3 or 4 define an integer row functional
  ell_{p;l_1,...,l_{n-2}}
to be the vacuum coefficient of
  partial_{l_1}...partial_{l_{n-2}} Bhat_p
acting on the canonical n-particle occupation basis, where
Bhat_p=sum_{a=0}^p partial_a partial_{p-a}. Its source weight is
M=p+sum l_i. Integer occupation multiplicities are included exactly.

All the following Kpoly matrices are QUADRATIC FORMS in the polynomial
coordinates (1), already including the physical metric. The channel's
three-body form is

 sum_a C_0a (h_a/e_t)
   [ell_{t;i}^T ell_{p;j}+ell_{p;j}^T ell_{t;i}]
 +sum_{a,b: i=i'} C_ab i!/(e_p e_{p'} d_j d_{j'})
   ell_{p;j}^T ell_{p';j'},                                  (2)

where b=(p',j'), i'=p'+j'-t. Its four-body form is

 sum_{a,b} C_ab h_a h_b
   ell_{p;j,i'}^T ell_{p';i,j'}.                             (3)

Each term is added only to its source weight: M=p+j for (2), and
M=p+j+i'=(p+j)+(p'+j')-t for (3). Summing channels gives the full
unaveraged forms, supported at M<=8 and M<=16 respectively.

To derive these coefficients rather than infer them numerically, note
that the physical functional a_l1...a_ls B_p after polynomial pullback is
  [kappa_p sqrt(l_1!...l_s!)/(e_p d_l1...d_ls)] ell_{p;l_1,...,l_s}.
Insert lambda=y_0/kappa_t and
alpha_{p,j}=y_{p,j} sqrt(i!/(kappa_p^2 j!)) in the exact normal-order
identities. The square-root factorials cancel, giving (2),(3) exactly.
This direct construction avoids subtracting large 2/3-body lifts from
the full four-particle square.

For the Hamiltonian, if Bhat_p now denotes its rectangular canonical
coordinate matrix from n particles to n-2, then

  Hpoly_{n,M}(x)=sum_{p=0}^M
       (kappa_p^2/e_p^2) Bhat_p^T G_{n-2,M-p}(x) Bhat_p.     (4)

The three-body target form is
  S3poly=Hpoly G_{3,M}^{-1} Hpoly-Hpoly.                     (5)
For n=4, let v_p=Bhat_{M-p} Bhat_p be the row to the vacuum. Then
  S4poly=sum_{p=0}^M
       [kappa_p^2 kappa_{M-p}^2/(e_p^2 e_{M-p}^2)] v_p^T v_p. (6)
The latter metric factors cancel between successive pair maps. The actual
four-body target multiplies (6) by 1-c9-mu, with the same frozen c9,mu.

3. Exact descendant and Schur formulas
--------------------------------------
In polynomial coordinates the rescaled spherical lowering operator is
  R(x)=sum_j (1-jx) x_{j+1} partial_j.                       (7)
For a fixed rational highest frame U_active, put C_0=U_active and
C_k=R(x)^k U_active. The squared normalization of the k-th descendant is
  n_k(x)=product_{l=1}^k l[r-(2z+l-1)x].                    (8)
The exact averaged form on this highest frame is
  Kbar_{r,z}(x)=[(2+x)/(r+(1-2z)x)]
       sum_{M=z}^{D_r} C_{M-z}^T Kpoly_{r,M}(x) C_{M-z}/n_{M-z}(x). (9)
These are forms on a fixed, generally nonorthonormal complement. The
Schur identity remains valid because normalized descendants preserve the
highest-frame Gram matrix. The comparison matrix finally tested is

 F_{r,z}(x)=U_active^T [Targetpoly_{r,z}(x)+delta_{r,z}Hpoly_{r,z}(x)]
                       U_active-Kbar_{r,z}(x).             (10)

The identities above reduce every entry to a finite expression in rational
constants, x, addition, multiplication, inversion, and positive square root.
All denominator ranges and square-root domains are checked by interval
operations. For the fluxes used here these domains are well inside their
positive regions.

4. Integer interval arithmetic and Taylor models
------------------------------------------------
An interval is [lo,hi]/2^192 with INTEGER endpoints. Addition and integer
scaling are exact; products and quotients round lower endpoints down and
upper endpoints up using integer floor/ceiling division. Square roots use
integer isqrt on endpoint*2^192, with an increment on the upper endpoint
unless its square is exact. No floating-point value enters any bound.

At chosen Taylor order m (default 3), a Jet encloses
  f(x)=sum_{k=0}^m c_k x^k + x^(m+1) r_f(x),              (11)
where every c_k and all values of r_f on [0,X], X=1/Qmin, belong to their
stored dyadic intervals. The interval for x has lower endpoint zero and
an outward-rounded upper endpoint at least X. Coefficients are formal
Taylor coefficients; interval width from arithmetic rounding is included.

Addition is componentwise. If P_f,P_g are the Taylor polynomials, the
product remainder is EXACTLY
  truncation_tail(P_f P_g)/x^(m+1)
       +P_f r_g+P_g r_f+x^(m+1)r_f r_g.                    (12)
The script evaluates this with interval Horner arithmetic, and convolves
the lower coefficients in the usual way.

For inversion, its Taylor coefficients b_k obey
  b_0=1/c_0,
  b_k=-(1/c_0)sum_{i=1}^k c_i b_{k-i}.
If P_b is this polynomial, the inverse remainder is
  r_{1/f}=-r_{f P_b}/f.                                   (13)
Indeed the polynomial part of f P_b is exactly one. A possibly nonzero
interval enclosure of its canceled lower coefficients is harmless: these
are exact algebraic cancellations for the actual coefficients.

For the positive square root, coefficients b_k satisfy
  b_0=sqrt(c_0),
  b_k=[c_k-sum_{i=1}^{k-1}b_i b_{k-i}]/(2b_0).
With P_b the resulting polynomial, the root remainder is exactly
  r_{sqrt(f)}=(r_f-r_{P_b^2})/(sqrt(f)+P_b).                (14)
This follows by rationalizing sqrt(f)-P_b. The relevant denominator is
checked not to contain zero. Thus (12)--(14) prove by structural induction
that every final matrix entry is enclosed for the WHOLE interval.

5. Rigorous matrix positivity on the whole interval
---------------------------------------------------
Symmetrize the final Taylor model by (F+F^T)/2; this leaves the exact form
unchanged. Write
  F(x)=sum_{i=0}^m A_i x^i+x^(m+1)R(x).
The program obtains the rigorous Euclidean operator-norm bound
  gamma=X^(m+1) max_i sum_j max(|lo(R_ij)|,|hi(R_ij)|),      (15)
rounded upward. It is valid because each R(x) is real symmetric and its
spectral norm is bounded by its maximum absolute row sum.

Put s=x/X in [0,1]. The Bernstein control matrices for its polynomial part
are exactly
  B_k=sum_{i=0}^k binom(k,i)/binom(m,i) X^i A_i,
  sum_i A_i x^i=sum_{k=0}^m binom(m,k)s^k(1-s)^(m-k)B_k.   (16)
The weights on the right are nonnegative and sum to one. The verifier
checks B_k-gamma I positive definite with interval LDL elimination. Each
pivot interval must have strictly positive lower endpoint. Since every
actual symmetric matrix is enclosed, positive pivots prove positivity
without a numerical eigenvalue tolerance.

If a control matrix is too conservative, de Casteljau subdivision divides
the parameter interval in half and produces the exact new control matrices
by repeated arithmetic averaging. The same gamma remains valid. A pass
requires all leaves to pass and hence covers the entire interval. Failure
after a bounded depth reports NOT CERTIFIED and returns a nonzero status;
it does not weaken the requested assertion silently.

Equations (15),(16) imply F(x)>=0 everywhere. Exact annihilation of the
fixed kernel extends positivity from the selected complement to the full
highest space, and rotational invariance extends it to all descendants.

6. Reproducibility and audit data
---------------------------------
The result JSON records Qmin, Taylor order, precision, the exact rational
highest frames, all coefficient/remainder intervals, all Bernstein/LDL
pivot intervals, each exact rational shift, and the final status. Printed
decimal diagnostics are conveniences only. They do not enter decisions.

The flags --body and --z are for debugging selected blocks; their result
explicitly says 'selected blocks only'. A whole-head result uses neither.
The scripts use only the standard library. No NumPy eigenvalue, numerical
SVD kernel, optimizer, or floating rounding mode enters certification.

This is a computer-assisted proof with a small auditable interval engine,
not a proof-assistant formalization. Its direct body formulas and interval
arithmetic have to be audited as mathematical code, just as the earlier
rational certificate did.
