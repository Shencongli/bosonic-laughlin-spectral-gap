RESULT

The original coherent comparisons are valid simultaneously for all integer
Q >= 4505, uniformly in total particle number N:

  R_{3,8,Q} <= 35(S_{4,Q}+H_Q),
  R_{4,16,Q} <= 2100(2S_{4,Q}+H_Q).

Optional constants and separately certified all-Q thresholds are:

  r   cutoff D   coefficient   sufficient Q   theta
  3      8          35             4505       1/14
  3      8          36             1254       1/7
  3      8          38              544       1/5
  4     16        2100             4330       1/12
  4     16        2200             2107       1/8

For r=3 the right-hand form is S4+H; for r=4 it is 2S4+H.
These are rigorous sufficient thresholds from the inequalities below, not
claims about optimal physical thresholds. This proves only the coherent
comparison lemma, independently of the finite certificate and gap theorem.

The main change is to take the coherent-kernel exponent M=aQ as a real
number, without rounding. This is allowed: the kernel is nonnegative for
every real M>=0, and the exact spectator marginal is (Q+1)/(M+1).
Nothing in the positive-lift bounds requires M to be an integer. We use
a=1/12 for three bodies and a=1/16 for four bodies. This choice is local
to these auxiliary comparison kernels and does not modify H_Q.

1. EXACT HIGHEST-SPACE AND COHERENT-OVERLAP IDENTIFICATION

Let r=3 or 4, h=r-2, and E_{r,Q}=V_Q tensor Sym^h U_Q. The oscillator
comparison space has one pair mode u and h spectator modes v_s, symmetric
under spectator interchange. Put d=h+1. At total oscillator degree z<=Q,
identify normalized occupation labels (p;j_1,...,j_h), p+sum j_s=z, with
the normalized spherical auxiliary weight-z basis.

The oscillator CM annihilator is

  a_zeta=(sqrt(2)a_u+sum_s a_{v_s})/sqrt(r).

Let F_z be any isometry onto its degree-z kernel, from a fixed multiplicity
coordinate space. Define the positive diagonal D_{Q,z} by

  D_{Q,z}(p;j_1,...,j_h)
   =sqrt[(2Q)_p/(2Q)^p] product_s sqrt[(Q)_{j_s}/Q^{j_s}].        (1)

The exact raising identity J_+/sqrt(Q)=D_{Q,z-1}^{-1}
(sqrt(r)a_zeta)D_{Q,z} shows that V_z=D_{Q,z}^{-1}F_z spans the exact
spherical highest space. Normalize with G_z=V_z^*V_z and frame
F_{z,Q}=V_z G_z^{-1/2}. The product inequality product(1-x)>=1-sum x
gives, for Z_z=z(z-1)/2 and Q>Z_z,

  (1-Z_z/Q)I <= D_{Q,z}^2 <= I,
  I <= G_z <= (1-Z_z/Q)^{-1}I,
  ||G_z^{-1/2}w||^2 >= (1-Z_z/Q)||w||^2.                       (2)

Here w is a vector in multiplicity coordinates. The first bound follows
because the sum of the losses in D^2 is
[p(p-1)/4 + sum_s j_s(j_s-1)/2]/Q <=z(z-1)/(2Q).

Use the stereographic coherent coordinate alpha on the sphere, with
unit-mass measure dOmega=d^2 alpha/[pi(1+|alpha|^2)^2]. A normalized
spin-capacity-L coherent vector is

  |alpha>_L=(1+|alpha|^2)^(-L/2)
               sum_{i=0}^L sqrt[binom(L,i)] alpha^i |i>.

Rescale the pair coordinate by u=sqrt(2Q)alpha and each spectator by
v_s=sqrt(Q)beta_s. Write x=(u,v_1,...,v_h) in C^d and

  X=|u|^2+sum_s|v_s|^2,
  d_s=|v_s-u/sqrt(2)|^2,
  Drel=sum_s d_s.

These are nonnegative dimensionless scalars. For r=3 or 4,

  Drel <= kappa X,   kappa=r/2.                                (3)

For r=3 the nonzero eigenvalue of this quadratic form is 3/2. For r=4,
in orthonormal relative coordinates it is |xi_1|^2+2|xi_2|^2.

Let psi=F_z G_z^{-1/2}w, and define its coherent-overlap polynomial
P(x)=sum_n conjugate(psi_n) x^n/sqrt(n!), a homogeneous degree-z
polynomial. Its complex-conjugated coefficients follow the bra-overlap
convention; its Gaussian norm is ||psi||. In a
coherent overlap, the D_{Q,z}^{-1} in the spherical highest vector
EXACTLY cancels the falling-factorial factors in the coherent expansion.
The quadratic form of A_{r,Q} on the normalized highest vector is
therefore exactly

  <w,A_{r,z,Q}w>
      = C_Q integral_{C^d} |P(x)|^2 W_Q(x) product_i d^2x_i/pi,

  C_Q=(1+1/(2Q))(1+1/Q)^h,
  W_Q= product_i (1+|x_i|^2/(c_i Q))^{-(c_i Q+2)}
                 product_s (1-y_s)^{aQ},
  c_0=2, c_s=1 (s>=1),
  y_s=d_s/[Q(1+|u|^2/(2Q))(1+|v_s|^2/Q)].                     (4)

This follows directly from the stereographic measures and the exact
identity 1-s(alpha,beta)=|alpha-beta|^2/
[(1+|alpha|^2)(1+|beta|^2)]. In particular 0<=y_s<=1. The spectator
symmetrizer gives no further factor: the highest vector is symmetric,
so its overlap with a symmetrized product equals its overlap with the
unsymmetrized product. The factor C_Q includes ALL dimension factors and
Jacobian factors.

The corresponding exact planar quadratic form uses

  W_infinity(x)=exp[-X-a Drel].                                (5)

It is the already evaluated oscillator coherent kernel: the CM factor
is the identity and the relative factors are thermal Gaussian operators.

2. A GLOBAL POLYNOMIAL LOWER BOUND FOR THE SPHERICAL WEIGHT

Choose 0<theta<1 and put

  b=a kappa^2/[2(1-theta)].                                    (6)

First consider X<=theta Q/kappa. Then y_s<=d_s/Q<=theta. For
0<=y<=theta, summing the logarithmic series gives

  -log(1-y) <= y+y^2/[2(1-theta)].

Also log(1+t)<=t. Applying these two scalar inequalities to (4), using
sum_s d_s^2 <= Drel^2 <=kappa^2 X^2, yields

  W_Q >= W_infinity exp[-(2X+bX^2)/Q]
      >= W_infinity [1-(2X+bX^2)/Q].                           (7)

The single-particle measure loss here is
2 sum_i |x_i|^2/(c_i Q)<=2X/Q. For the interaction weight,
Qy_s<=d_s and Qy_s^2<=d_s^2/Q. All signs in (7) consequently give a
lower bound, without using a truncated asymptotic series.

For X>theta Q/kappa the bracket in (7) is nonpositive provided

  Q >= Qloc := 2(1-theta)/(a theta^2),                           (8)

because b(theta Q/kappa)^2/Q>=1. Since W_Q>=0, (7) is then valid
for EVERY x in C^d. This is the step that removes any radial-tail or
descendant-tail estimate entirely. Even at zeros of a coherent overlap,
the outside-region bound remains valid.

3. EXACT GAUSSIAN MOMENT CONTROL

For any nonzero homogeneous polynomial P of degree z, normalize the
positive measure |P|^2 W_infinity product_i d^2x_i/pi to mass one.
In polar coordinates x=sqrt(X)omega, omega on the unit sphere in C^d,
its conditional radial distribution at fixed omega is Gamma with integer
shape k=z+d and rate lambda(omega)=1+a Drel(omega)>=1:

  const(omega) X^{k-1} exp[-lambda(omega)X] dX.

Consequently its exact conditional moments satisfy E[X|omega]<=k and
E[X^2|omega]<=k(k+1). Averaging in omega preserves these bounds. Thus
integrating the global inequality (7) gives

  integral |P|^2 W_Q
     >= [1-c_z/Q] integral |P|^2 W_infinity,
  c_z=2(z+d)+b(z+d)(z+d+1).                                   (9)

No unproved isotropy of P or of the relative Gaussian is assumed: the
Gamma argument is conditional on the angular direction. The inequality
is also true for P=0.

Let the retained cutoff be D=8 for r=3 and D=16 for r=4. Put

  Z=D(D-1)/2,
  k=D+d,
  c=2k+b k(k+1).

For Q>max(Z,c) and Q>=max(D,Qloc), equations (2), (4), and (9) imply

  A_{r,z,Q} >= C_Q (1-Z/Q)(1-c/Q) t_r I,  0<=z<=D,             (10)

where t_r is the smallest retained planar eigenvalue. The known exact
Gaussian evaluations give

  t_3=(8/9)^9,
  t_4=(16/17)(8/9)^17.                                       (11)

The inequalities hold on all multiplicities. Spin invariance and kernel
positivity make the corresponding projector comparison valid on the
whole auxiliary space, as in the previous coherent proof.

4. POSITIVE LIFT, EXACT RATIONAL COEFFICIENT, AND ALL-Q MONOTONICITY

The exact spectator marginal is mu_Q=(Q+1)/(aQ+1). Compose the
projector bound from (10) with W_r,W_r^*, apply the positive r-body lift,
and use the exact occupation estimates already established in the draft.
The resulting coefficient multiplying S4+H (r=3) or 2S4+H (r=4) is
at most

  C_r(Q) := C_{r,infinity}/D_r(1/Q),
  C_{r,infinity}=1/(a^h t_r),
  D_r(x)=(1+x/a)^h(1+x/2)(1-Zx)(1-cx).                        (12)

This keeps the useful exact cancellation
mu_Q^h/C_Q=a^{-h}/[(1+1/(aQ))^h(1+1/(2Q))].
For each parameter choice in the table, Z+c>h/a+1/2. For
0<=x<min(1/Z,1/c), logarithmic differentiation gives

  D_r'(x)/D_r(x)
    =h/(a+x)+1/(2+x)-Z/(1-Zx)-c/(1-cx)
    <=h/a+1/2-Z-c<0.                                         (13)

Therefore C_r(Q) is decreasing for every Q in the permitted range.
Checking the exact rational inequality at a threshold Q0 proves it for
ALL larger integer Q. This is not a sampled-Q extrapolation.

The five table rows have the following c values:

  (r,target,theta,c,Q0)
  (3, 35,  1/14, 3235/104, 4505),
  (3, 36,  1/7,  1025/32,  1254),
  (3, 38,  1/5,  2105/64,   544),
  (4,2100, 1/12, 988/11,   4330),
  (4,2200, 1/8,  646/7,    2107).

The exact Fraction checker optimized_coherent_check.py verifies all
domain conditions, Q0>=Qloc, the strict sign needed for (13), and
C_r(Q0)<=target. It also verifies C_r(Q0-1)>target for the same theta,
which only proves local optimality of the integer threshold within that
specific bound and parameter choice. No floating value enters a proof.

All estimates are on auxiliary few-body coefficients before lifting.
Thus their thresholds are uniform in the total physical particle number.

5. GENERAL a AND DEGREE-RESOLVED ERROR ABSORPTION

The preceding proof remains valid for ANY fixed real a>0; rational a
makes the final scalar certificate entirely rational. For a single kernel,
a must be the same for all its spin blocks. Its exact lowest limiting
eigenvalues at relative degree z are

  t_{3,z}(a)=(1+3a/2)^(-z-1),
  t_{4,z}(a)=(1+a)^(-1)(1+2a)^(-z-1).                         (14)

For each z put Z_z=z(z-1)/2, k_z=z+r-1, and choose any proof parameter
0<theta_z<1 satisfying Q0>=2(1-theta_z)/(a theta_z^2). Define

  b_z=a(r/2)^2/[2(1-theta_z)],
  c_z=2k_z+b_z k_z(k_z+1),
  C_{r,z}(Q)=1/{a^h t_{r,z}(a)
      (1+1/(aQ))^h (1+1/(2Q))(1-Z_z/Q)(1-c_z/Q)}.             (15)

All conditions are Q>=z, Q>max(Z_z,c_z), and the displayed condition
on theta_z. Choosing theta_z differently across blocks does not change
the kernel: theta is only used to prove a lower bound on its positive
weight. Consequently, if delta_z>=0 and a physical few-body error obeys

  E_{r,Q} >= -sum_{z=0}^D delta_z H_{r,Q} Pi^{phys}_{z,Q},

then its negative contribution after lifting is bounded by

  max_{0<=z<=D}[delta_z C_{r,z}(Q)] times (S4+H)       (r=3),
  max_{0<=z<=D}[delta_z C_{r,z}(Q)] times (2S4+H)      (r=4).  (16)

Indeed, the single auxiliary weighted projector sum_z delta_z Pi_z is
bounded by max_z(delta_z/lambda_z) times the SAME positive kernel A,
where lambda_z is its degree-z lower bound from (10) with z-dependent
constants. W_r(sum_z delta_z Pi_z)W_r^*=sum_z delta_z H_r Pi_z,
and the positive lift gives (16). There is no sum over z in its scalar
cost. This argument includes all multiplicities in each spin block.

For a bound uniform in all Q>=Q0, small z needs care: C_{r,z}(Q) is not
always decreasing. A general exact bound valid for every z is

  Cbar_{r,z}(Q0)=max{C_{r,z}(Q0), 1/[a^h t_{r,z}(a)]}.          (17)

To prove it, the logarithm of
D_z(x)=(1+x/a)^h(1+x/2)(1-Z_z x)(1-c_z x) is concave on
0<=x<=1/Q0. Therefore log D_z(x)>=min(log D_z(0),log D_z(1/Q0));
since D_z(0)=1, its reciprocal is bounded by the maximum of the endpoint
reciprocals. If Z_z+c_z>h/a+1/2, (13) also proves the simpler monotone
bound C_{r,z}(Q)<=C_{r,z}(Q0).

Thus (16) holds uniformly with max_z delta_z Cbar_{r,z}(Q0), when the
delta_z themselves are uniform error bounds for Q>=Q0. If the errors
are Q-dependent, their dependence must separately be retained or bounded.
